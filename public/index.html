FAQ

Before your consultation

Common questions answered to help you feel confident and prepared.

What is Contour Pro Max?

A multi-technology body contouring system combining vacuum roller, cavitation, RF, and infrared light for sculpting and skin improvement.

Is it suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability is determined during consultation based on health history, goals, and treatment expectations.

What should I expect from treatment?

A guided, professional experience with clear communication, realistic outcomes, and aftercare support. No downtime required.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your individual goals and the area being treated. Most patients see results in 4–8 sessions, spaced 1–2 weeks apart.

Is there any downtime?

There is no downtime required. You can resume normal activities immediately after each session. Most clients describe the treatment as relaxing.

How soon will I see results?

Some patients notice improvements after the first session. Optimal results typically develop over 6–12 weeks as the body naturally processes treated fat cells.
